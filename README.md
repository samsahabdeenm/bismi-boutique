# Bismi Boutique — Storefront Preview

Responsive static HTML/CSS/JavaScript fashion catalogue concept for Bismi Boutique.

## Brand
Magenta/berry pink, ivory and golden-yellow crescent accent inspired by the supplied Bismi Boutique logo.

## Catalogue
Women: three-piece sets, two-piece sets, shirts, kurtas, cotton wear, towels, burqa and burqa shawls.
Kids: girls' co-ord sets, boys' co-ord sets, T-shirts, track pants and socks. Each category includes six demo product entries.

## Features
- Responsive navigation and mobile menu
- Search and category filters
- Product cards with illustrative images and demo prices
- Favorites and bag saved in browser localStorage
- Category expansion, SEO metadata, Open Graph, ClothingStore JSON-LD, robots.txt, sitemap.xml and llms.txt
- Contact, privacy, terms and shipping/returns pages

## Before production
The logo SVG is a web-friendly interpretation inspired by the supplied logo, not a pixel-perfect extraction. Product names, remote illustrative images and prices are placeholders. Replace them with approved catalogue data; confirm official domain, DNS and GitHub Pages custom-domain settings. Add official contact and social details. Connect real ordering, inventory, payments and newsletter flows before accepting orders. Review legal text against actual business practices and applicable law.
